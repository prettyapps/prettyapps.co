Privacy Policy

Pause Point is designed with privacy as a foundation, not an afterthought. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and why the answer to both is: almost nothing.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone
  • All data stays on your device
  • Apple Health data is written locally — we never read it back

Who we are

Pause Point is developed and published by Pretty Apps LLC, a limited liability company registered in Georgia (country). You can reach us at contact@prettyapps.co.

What we collect

Nothing. Pause Point does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app operates entirely on your device.

Data stored on your device

Pause Point stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your timer preferences (duration, interval bell, warmup settings)
  • Your appearance preferences (theme, color mode)
  • Your session history (dates and durations of completed sessions)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Apple Health integration

If you choose to enable Apple Health integration, Pause Point will write completed meditation sessions to your Health app as Mindful Minutes. This data is stored by Apple under their privacy policy and is never transmitted to us. We do not read, access, or process any Health data.

Apple Watch

The Pause Point Apple Watch companion app communicates with the iPhone app only to synchronize timer state. No data from this communication is collected or stored outside your devices.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting tools, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking in Pause Point. There are no SDKs embedded in the app beyond Apple's own frameworks.

Children's privacy

Pause Point does not collect information from anyone, including children. The app is appropriate for all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever update this policy, we will post the new version at this URL and update the effective date below. We will notify users through an app update if any material changes occur.

Contact

Questions about privacy? We're happy to answer. Email us at contact@prettyapps.co.
